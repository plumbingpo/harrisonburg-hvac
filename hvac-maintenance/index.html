The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

There is a short list of things homeowners can handle: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and checking the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded give no visible warning. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks just like a new one. Low charge shows up only in superheat and subcooling readings. Cracked heat exchangers, weak flame signals, marginal pressure switches, and amp draws closing in on the breaker's limit are all invisible until measured, and all of them cost less to fix before they fail.

On fuel-burning equipment, maintenance is also about safety. A failing burner, flame proving circuit, vent, or draft problem can put combustion products where they should never be, which is why those items get instrumented verification every year. HVAC Maintenance Prevention Tips

A few simple habits now, like regular filter changes and seasonal tune-ups, can add years to your system's life. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Swap the filter every one to three months, the most valuable habit a homeowner can build
  • Keep the outdoor unit clear of vegetation and rinse the coil gently each spring
  • If you have gas or other fuel-burning equipment, test your CO detectors every month
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
  • Jot down any unusual sounds or run times, since they help your technician diagnose early
  • Clear the condensate drain early in cooling season, before algae has a chance to grow
  • Get your tune-ups on the calendar during mild weather, well before the rush
